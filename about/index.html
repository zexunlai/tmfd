About

Ze Xun Lai

Editor, publisher, and writer of TMFD.

TMFD begins with a simple proposition: the things we care about deserve to be listened to with attention, context, and room to change.

01 / Publication

TMFD

TMFD — Task Force on Music-related Financial Disclosures is an independent, open-access music magazine founded and edited by Ze Xun Lai. Published in English and Traditional Chinese, it follows music through sound, artistic expression, culture, geography, and environment.

Its name borrows the language of disclosure not as a reporting standard, but as an editorial method: to make visible the cultural, emotional, spatial, and social values held in a song, a performance, an instrument, or a listening room. TMFD values context over rankings, curiosity over fixed boundaries, and sustained attention over novelty alone.

02 / Biography

Autobiography

Ze Xun Lai is a sustainability consultant based in Taipei. With a background in Geographic Data Science from National Taiwan University, his professional work moves across climate, biodiversity, circular economy, and the information systems that help organisations make complex questions traceable, verifiable, and useful.

TMFD is a parallel editorial practice: a place where that interest in systems meets a long-standing attention to music. It treats listening as more than consumption — as a way to notice relationships between sound, place, memory, technology, and the people who make a scene possible.

03 / Interests

Interest

Listening and music

Independent music, arrangement, instrumental performance, live rooms, and the small material details that make a recording stay present after its release week.

Work and research

Sustainability systems, environmental data, circular economy, climate and nature frameworks, and the careful translation of complex evidence into work that people can use.
